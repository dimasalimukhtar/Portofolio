# BLDC Motor Load Characterization on Micro Electric Vehicle (MEVi)

## 1. Project Overview

This research investigates the loading characteristics of a Brushless Direct Current (BLDC) motor installed in a Micro Electric Vehicle (MEVi).

The study focuses on the relationship between operating conditions and vehicle acceleration, braking behavior, electrical power consumption, and energy requirements.

This research was conducted as part of an electrical engineering research internship at the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN) and supports the development of an engineering-based battery sizing recommendation.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Analyze the electrical characteristics of the BLDC motor under different operating conditions.
- Investigate acceleration and braking behavior under varying loads and road gradients.
- Evaluate voltage, current, electrical power, and energy consumption.
- Analyze motor loading characteristics during vehicle operation.
- Develop a battery sizing recommendation based on the experimental results.

## 3. Research Parameters

The experimental study considers the following independent variables:

| Parameter | Experimental Levels |
|---|---|
| Vehicle load | 50 kg, 65 kg, 80 kg |
| Road gradient | -5°, 0°, +5° |
| Braking-test speed | 6 km/h, 13 km/h, 18 km/h |
| Acceleration-test interval | 6–9 km/h, 9–13 km/h, 13–18 km/h |

The braking-test speed levels and acceleration-test intervals are treated as separate experimental factors.

## 4. Experimental Methodology

Response Surface Methodology (RSM) with a Box–Behnken Design (BBD) approach is used to structure the experimental investigation.

The experimental work includes:

- Acceleration testing under different load and road-gradient conditions.
- Braking testing at selected vehicle speeds.
- Repeated measurements to evaluate experimental consistency.
- Recording electrical parameters during vehicle operation.
- Processing experimental data for statistical and engineering analysis.

## 5. Electrical Parameters

The electrical measurements include:

- Voltage (V)
- Current (A)
- Electrical power (W)
- Energy consumption (Wh)
- Battery consumption

The analysis also considers mechanical power and motor efficiency where the required mechanical measurements are available.

## 6. Data Analysis

The analysis is intended to examine the relationship between experimental factors and the measured responses.

Potential analysis activities include:

- Data preprocessing and calculation of electrical energy consumption.
- Response surface modeling.
- Analysis of variance (ANOVA).
- Regression model evaluation.
- Examination of acceleration and braking characteristics.
- Estimation of battery energy requirements.

Numerical findings and statistical conclusions will be added after the experimental data have been processed and validated.

## 7. Battery Sizing

The battery sizing recommendation will be developed using the measured energy consumption and relevant vehicle operating requirements.

The assessment will distinguish between measured energy consumption, estimated energy requirements, and the proposed battery configuration.

Any final capacity recommendation must be supported by the experimental results and the applicable battery design assumptions.
