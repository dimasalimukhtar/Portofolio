## 6. Electrical Engineering Calculation Standards

Engineering calculations should document:

- Given parameters and assumptions.
- Applicable equations.
- Units and unit conversions.
- Calculation steps.
- Results and engineering interpretation.
- Applicable standards or references, where verified.

Calculations intended for practical design must be checked against applicable regulations, engineering standards, equipment specifications, and project requirements.

## 7. Technical Documentation Principles

Each detailed engineering note should include:

1. Title and purpose.
2. Scope and assumptions.
3. Technical background.
4. Calculation or analysis method.
5. Worked example or supporting diagram.
6. Results and interpretation.
7. References and limitations.
